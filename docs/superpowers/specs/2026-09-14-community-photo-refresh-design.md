## Quality and framing

- Preserve authentic photographs. No AI regeneration, compositing, facial changes, false sharpening claims, or removal of cultural content.
- Use original sources where available. Consistent delivery settings do not make unequal source resolution identical: the existing first three are 1600px display assets; the student photograph is only 1280px wide. Do not upscale this file and describe it as an original-resolution upgrade.
- Produce responsive browser-optimized assets without enlargement, preserving original aspect ratios. Target up to 1600px on the long edge for carousel delivery and appropriate 800px/1600px derivatives for page panels, bounded by source dimensions. Keep originals and hashes in the source/provenance record.
- Preserve the existing single-image slide presentation and navy fades. Portrait slides retain contain framing; landscape crops must keep Morales and pictured faces visible at narrow, tablet, and desktop widths. Prefer contain if cover would remove a face.
- Keep the volunteer office group fully visible; the existing 4:5 panel must not crop the people at either edge. Use full-image containment or an asset-specific natural ratio within the existing card width rather than enlarging its crop.
- The official About/home portrait stays unchanged; never reinstate the previously rejected `professional-about-primary` photograph just because of its filename.
